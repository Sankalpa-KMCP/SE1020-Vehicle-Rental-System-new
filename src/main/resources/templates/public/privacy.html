Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026. This policy describes how DriveAway handles personal data for this application.

1. Data we collect

We collect account details you provide (name, email, phone, username) and booking-related information (vehicle choice, dates, payment references where applicable) needed to operate the rental service.

2. How we use data

Data is used to authenticate users, fulfil reservations, communicate about bookings, and improve our service. Administrative staff may access booking and customer records for support and compliance.

3. Storage

In this deployment, records may be stored in local data files under configured application paths. Protect your device and credentials; do not share passwords.

4. Your choices

You may request corrections to your profile through customer support. You can stop using the service and delete local sessions by signing out or clearing browser data.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: contact us.
