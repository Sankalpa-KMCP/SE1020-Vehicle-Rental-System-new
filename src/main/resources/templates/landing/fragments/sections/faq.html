You must provide a valid Sri Lankan National Identity Card (NIC) or Passport, along with a valid Driver's License that matches the vehicle class you are renting.

Yes. A refundable security deposit is held on your credit or debit card at the time of pickup. The amount varies based on the vehicle class (e.g., Luxury vs Economy).

You can cancel your reservation for free up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup time. Cancellations made within 24 hours of pickup will incur a fee equivalent to one rental day.

All DriveAway vehicles are fully insured. In the event of an accident, immediately ensure your safety, notify local authorities, and then call our 24/7 hotline. We will dispatch a replacement vehicle and handle the insurance claims.

Standard rentals include a generous daily mileage limit of 150km. Excess mileage is billed per kilometer. Unlimited mileage packages are available for select long-term rentals and luxury vehicles.
