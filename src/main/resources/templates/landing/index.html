FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to rent a vehicle?

A valid driving licence, a national ID or passport, and a payment method for your booking. Requirements are confirmed at checkout.

How do I book a car or motorcycle?

Browse our vehicles, choose your dates, and complete payment online. You will receive a confirmation email with pickup details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept secure card payments online. Cash payment options may be available for certain bookings — details are shown before you confirm.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes, subject to our rental terms. Contact us as soon as your plans change and we will do our best to help reschedule or cancel.

Is insurance included in the daily rate?

Coverage depends on the vehicle and hire type. Full details are displayed before you confirm your reservation.

Where can I pick up a vehicle?

Pickup locations are shown when you book. Bring your licence and booking confirmation to collect your vehicle at the agreed time.
