Browse the fleet, select your dates, and complete checkout. You will receive instant confirmation by email.

What payment methods are supported?

DriveAway supports secure card payments and cash settlement options, depending on your booking type.

Can fleet operators manage maintenance?

Yes. Operators use the dashboard to schedule service, track availability, and keep every vehicle road-ready.

Is insurance included?

Coverage varies by vehicle class. Full policy details are shown before you confirm your reservation.
