How to rent

Find a vehicle, confirm, and collect the keys

Hiring a car or motorcycle with DriveAway takes just a few steps — here’s how it works from start to finish.

1

Find a vehicle

Browse cars and motorcycles, filter by type and daily rate, and check availability for your dates.

2

Confirm & pay

Select your hire dates, pay securely online, and receive a confirmation email with your booking details.

3

Collect & return

Bring your driving licence and ID to pickup. Drive away, then return the vehicle at the end of your hire.
