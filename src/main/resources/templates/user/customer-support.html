Frequently asked questions

What happens if I return the vehicle late?

A grace period of 2 hours is provided. After that, an additional full day is charged. Contact support early if you expect a delay.

What do I do in case of an accident or breakdown?

Move to a safe location, then call emergency roadside assistance. Do not attempt repairs yourself. Obtain a police report if another vehicle is involved.

How do I cancel or modify my reservation?

Use My Bookings to edit or request cancellation. Cancellations 24 hours before pickup are fully refunded after staff approval.
